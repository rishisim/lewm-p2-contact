# Adaptive prediction paper status

Status: `paper_ready_bounded_prediction_claim`

This branch is the canonical record for the completed adaptive-prediction
research line. It combines the tracked V1--V5 development history with the
curated manuscript, Cube generalization result, PushT pilot and binary
confirmation, planning-boundary diagnostics, marginal-value analysis, paper
evidence synthesis, and independent V5 validity audit.

## Supported conclusion

A causal transition-level allocator can modestly improve next-latent
prediction at the same declared latent-prediction operation budget. The result
is confirmed on Cube and replicated with a PushT-specific binary policy.

## Boundaries

This branch does not claim downstream planning or control improvement,
wall-clock or energy savings, direct Cube-to-PushT transfer, universal
robustness, or literal end-to-end FLOP equality. The planning bridge is a
negative boundary result, and the independent audit supports V5 with recorded
caveats.

## Artifact policy

Git retains source, protocols, reports, decisions, compact tables, manifests,
and audit records. Raw episodes, per-transition arrays, caches, fault-copy
fixtures, rendered manuscript products, and other reproducible run outputs
remain outside Git.
