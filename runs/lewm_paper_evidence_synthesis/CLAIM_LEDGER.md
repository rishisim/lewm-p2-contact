# Claim ledger

## Conventions

- Unless stated otherwise, **benefit = comparator episode-averaged MSE minus adaptive episode-averaged MSE**, so positive values favor adaptive allocation.
- “Simultaneous LB” is the familywise one-sided lower bound specified by the study. “95% CI” denotes a two-sided percentile interval.
- Cube shift “fixed-whitened” uses the original PlanOracle-native whitening matrix without recalibration.
- Exact paths and SHA-256 hashes are collected in `EVIDENCE_INDEX.json`.

### Aggregate robustness statement

The frozen Cube shift study supports **partial zero-shot robustness**: four of six regime-by-endpoint simultaneous lower bounds were positive. That aggregate statement is a summary of C4–C6 plus N2–N3 below, not a universal-generalization claim.
