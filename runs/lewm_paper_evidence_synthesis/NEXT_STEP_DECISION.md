# Next-step decision

## Decision: (a) begin full manuscript drafting now

No experiment is genuinely required before writing the paper defined by the present evidence. The central claim is already supported by two fresh confirmation cohorts with independent recomputation:

1. a frozen 1,600-episode Cube PlanOracle confirmation; and
2. a 240-episode PushT confirmation of a binary hypothesis derived from, but tested after, the consumed four-depth pilot.

The surrounding studies do what a mature paper needs them to do. The Cube shift study maps the claim's partial robustness; the formally negative PushT pilot prevents selective success narration; and the multi-step/planning decomposition prevents prediction fidelity from being overstated as control utility. More data would broaden the paper, but none is needed to make the bounded prediction-at-counted-compute claim internally coherent.

## Why the other choices are rejected

### (b) Run one bounded experiment first — rejected

A new environment, a stronger PushT policy, or a closed-loop planner would test a materially broader claim. Such work could be valuable, but it is not a missing validity check for the current claim. Proposing it as a prerequisite would turn “more external validity would be useful” into an endless stopping rule.

The only experiment that could become necessary is conditional on a changed paper objective: if the manuscript is reframed to claim control improvement, then a preregistered, adequately informative closed-loop study under a competent policy is required. That is a different central claim and should not delay the present predictive-compute paper.

### (c) Stop or reshape the claim — rejected, except for scope discipline

The claim should be narrowed, not abandoned. The evidence does not justify “general adaptive world-model efficiency,” “zero-shot cross-environment transfer,” or “faster control.” It does justify the more precise statement that causal learned allocation can improve next-latent prediction relative to strong transition-independent fixed-depth mixtures at matched counted FLOPs in the confirmed Cube and PushT settings.

## Stop rule

Begin writing now. Do not launch another experiment unless a specific reviewer-facing claim is added that the current evidence cannot answer and the new experiment has a predeclared decision consequence. “A second environment would be nice” or “control would be more exciting” does not meet that bar.
