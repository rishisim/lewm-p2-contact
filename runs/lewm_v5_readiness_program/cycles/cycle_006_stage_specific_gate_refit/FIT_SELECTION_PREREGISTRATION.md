# Cycle 006 fit/selection preregistration

Cycle 005 established that stricter thresholds alone do not make the original shared gate's deeper-stage ordering stable. Cycle 006 therefore makes one bounded architectural change: three stage-specific versions of the same dual affine minimax gate, each using the unchanged 1,046 counted causal features. The base model, refiner, native target whitening, feature graph, DGP, sparse semantics, comparators, numerical contract, and every discovery success criterion remain unchanged.

Before any cycle-006 episode, all generation, dense fit/selection evaluation, ridge fitting, candidate selection, gate compilation, sparse execution, prospective analysis, independent verification, latency, outcome mapping, and manifest code must be materialized and hash-sealed. Exactly 240 fresh fit episodes and 120 fresh out-of-fit selection episodes are assigned in advance. Every dense exit and causal stage feature is evaluated on both roles with only `action,pixels` loaded. Fit targets alone determine per-stage feature normalization and the three regularization-specific dual heads. Nine candidates are fixed by three ridge penalties and three fit-score quantiles. Selection is accessed only after all fit weights and thresholds exist, and no selected head is refit.

The fixed ranking and eligibility rules are machine-readable in `candidate_grid.json`. A selected candidate must then be frozen, compiled, independently checked on the complete fit/selection traces, and placed under a second pre-prospective seal. Only after that seal may 12 excluded smoke episodes and exactly 300 wholly fresh prospective episodes be generated. No sequential expansion, criterion revision, V3/HDF5 access, contact input, or V5 outcome generation is allowed.
